# Research questions

RQ1: Does multi-model consensus reduce factual error versus one model?
RQ2: Does claim-level comparison detect contradictions better than answer review?
RQ3: Does independent evidence improve aggregation?
RQ4: Can raw confidence be calibrated on unseen outcomes?
RQ5: Does reviewed knowledge help later questions without propagating stale corrections?
RQ6: What accuracy/coverage/latency/token/cost tradeoffs justify added complexity?

Use real, independently labeled questions for research conclusions. The 42 authored
synthetic registry examples exercise controllable failure paths and seven categories;
they do not measure model intelligence or establish statistical significance.
Pre-register splits and criteria. Separate first-seen facts from repeated-fact memory
experiments and from calibration labels. Report abstention coverage and conditional
accuracy alongside overall correctness. Keep all raw outputs and configurations.
